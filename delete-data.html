Privacy

Delete your Fold Fortress data

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version. Fold Fortress: Card Defense, published by Thomas Sobstyl (FoxTaming), has no account. Your progress is on your device and goes when you delete the app. To have the data held by the game's services deleted, send an email to thomas.sobstyl@me.com.

How to ask

  1. Write to thomas.sobstyl@me.com with the subject "Fold Fortress data deletion".
  2. Say whether you play on iPhone, iPad or Android.
  3. If you bought something in the game, add the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt. It is the only way I can find your purchase record, because the game never knows your name or email.

I answer within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Purchase records at RevenueCat (store receipts, purchase history, the anonymous app user identifier), found with your order number.
  • Analytics and crash data at Google Firebase, when it can be matched to your copy of the game. It is stored under app and installation identifiers, not under your name, so a match is not always possible; crash reports are deleted by themselves after 90 days.

What you can delete yourself, at once

  • Your progress and settings: delete the app. On Android, also remove the game from your Google account's device backup if you use it.
  • Your advertising identifier: reset or delete it in your device's privacy settings.
  • Your consent: change it in the game under Settings > Ad privacy options, where your region has that form.

What is kept

  • Apple and Google keep their own records of your payments, under your account with them. I cannot delete those.
  • Records I must keep for tax and accounting are kept for as long as the law requires, without any link to how you play.
  • Data Google already used to show and measure ads is handled under Google's privacy policy; you can manage it in your Google account or at adssettings.google.com.
