Privacy

Fold Fortress collects nothing.

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version. Fold Fortress: Card Defense does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK that sends data anywhere. The game makes no network request to send information about you.

What is collected

Nothing. Not your name, not your email, not an anonymous identifier, not a device fingerprint, not your location, and not usage statistics. The Google Play data safety section says No data collected and No data shared, because there is nothing to collect.

Where your progress lives

  • Your progress, runs, collections and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • On Android, the system's own backup to your Google account may include that saved game, if you have device backup turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your account; I cannot read it.
  • Deleting the app deletes the local data.

Purchases and ads

The current version has no in-app purchases and shows no ads yet. Ads are planned: a coming version will show ads from an advertising network, which may use your device's advertising identifier to deliver and measure them. Before that version ships, this page will name the network, list what it collects and how you can limit it (for example by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings), and the store privacy forms will be updated to match.

Children

Fold Fortress is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, deletion and portability apply to data a company holds about you. I hold none. If you would like confirmation of that in writing, ask and I will provide it.

This page

This page is a static file hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookie and runs no analytics. GitHub keeps standard server logs as the hosting provider, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version appears on this page with a new date above.
