Reporting a bug

To help us fix it quickly, please include:

  • your phone or tablet model and its iOS or Android version;
  • what you were doing and what happened (a screenshot or a short screen recording helps a lot);
  • the realm and battle you were in, if the bug happened during a run.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Fold Fortress plays fully offline.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, unless your device's own backup restores it.

How do I learn the game again?
Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Replay tutorial: Pip teaches the first battle again, and your runs and progress stay as they are.
